Questions

Is DukaanKit really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no limits, and the small credit line on posters can be switched off. It is made by an independent web developer from Visakhapatnam who also builds full websites for shops.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open a tool, fill in your details and download. Nothing is uploaded.

Which languages are supported on the posters?

English, Hindi and Telugu, alone or mixed (English + Hindi, English + Telugu).

Can I use it on a basic Android phone?

Yes. The pages are small and fast, and work in Chrome on budget Android phones. You can print directly or send the PDF to a print shop on WhatsApp.
