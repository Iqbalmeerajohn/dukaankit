How to make a festival offer poster

  1. Pick the festival. If yours is not listed, choose "Other" and type its name.
  2. Type your shop name and the offer in a few words. Put the conditions (minimum bill, which items) in the second line so nobody feels cheated at the counter.
  3. Add the offer dates. Customers trust an offer more when they can see when it ends.
  4. Download the PDF to print it (A4 for the window or shutter, A5 for the counter), or the PNG to send on WhatsApp and post on Instagram or Facebook.

Tip: one clear offer beats five small ones. "Flat 20% off on sarees" is read from across the road; a list of prices is not.

Closing for the festival too? Make a matching holiday notice. Want customers to pay by UPI during the rush? Print a UPI QR standee.

Questions

Is this festival poster maker really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark except a small credit line you can switch off, and no limit on how many posters you make.

Can I share the poster on WhatsApp?

Yes. Tap "Download PNG" and share the image from your gallery to WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook. The PDF is for printing.

Can I write the offer in Hindi or Telugu?

Yes. The festival name, dates and wishes are written for you in the language you choose under Poster language. The offer and conditions are printed exactly as you type them, in any language.

Which festivals are included?

Diwali, Sankranti, Ugadi, Holi, Eid, Bakrid, Ganesh Chaturthi, Dussehra, Christmas, New Year, Republic Day and Independence Day. Choose "Other" for any festival, or "No festival" for a general sale.

What should I write so the offer is honest?

Write only an offer you will really give at the counter, and put the conditions on the poster. If the discount is only on some items or above a bill amount, say so. It saves arguments and brings customers back.

Do I need internet?

Only to open the page the first time. After that it works offline, and nothing you type leaves your phone.
