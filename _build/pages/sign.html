How to print your shop sign

  1. Pick the sign type, type your shop name, and choose the language your customers read.
  2. For a door sign, choose A4 landscape. For a counter or window, A5 is enough.
  3. Download the PDF and print it. For Open/Closed, print both pages and paste them back to back, or laminate them and hang on a string.
  4. For holidays, print the notice a few days early and stick it on the shutter so regular customers know in advance.

Ink saving tip: the White colour option uses very little ink and still looks clean on a door.

Questions

Can I make a sign only in Telugu or only in Hindi?

Yes. Choose తెలుగు or हिन्दी under Poster language. You can also mix English with either language.

My festival is not in the list. What do I do?

Choose "Other" and type the name in English, and optionally in Hindi and Telugu. If you leave those empty, the English name is used.

Can I show different timings on Sunday?

Yes. In Shop timings, every day has its own opening and closing time, and you can untick a day to show it as closed.

Will the Hindi and Telugu text print correctly?

Yes. The poster is made as an image inside your browser, so the letters print exactly as you see them in the preview, even on printers without Indian language fonts.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. It also works without internet once the page has loaded.
