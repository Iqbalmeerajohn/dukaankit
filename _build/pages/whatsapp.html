How to use your WhatsApp link

  1. Enter the number that has WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, without the 0 at the start.
  2. Write a short starting message, for example "I want to order" or "Please send today's price list".
  3. Tap Test the link on your phone to check it opens the right chat.
  4. Copy the link for your Instagram bio, Google Business Profile and visiting cards. Print the poster for the shop.

Questions

What is a wa.me link?

It is WhatsApp's official click-to-chat link. Anyone who opens it gets a chat with your number, even if they have not saved your contact.

Do I need WhatsApp Business?

No, it works with normal WhatsApp too. WhatsApp Business adds a catalogue, quick replies and away messages, which help when many customers message you.

Can I write the message in Hindi or Telugu?

Yes. Type the message in any language. It is encoded safely in the link and shows up correctly in WhatsApp.

Will my number be visible to everyone?

Yes, anyone who scans or opens the link sees your number, the same as a number painted on a board. Use a shop number if you want to keep your personal number private.

Is my number stored anywhere?

Only in your own browser, so the form is filled next time. DukaanKit has no server and sends nothing.
